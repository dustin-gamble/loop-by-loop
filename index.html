    What you need
    
    
    Stitch key
    
      
    
        
    MR
    Magic ring: an adjustable loop you crochet into, then pull closed.
    
        
    sc
    Single crochet: insert hook, pull up a loop, yarn over, pull through both loops.
    
        
    inc
    Increase: two single crochets in the same stitch.
    
        
    dec
    Invisible decrease: hook through the front loops of the next two stitches, then finish as one single crochet.
    
        
    ( ) ×6
    Work everything in the brackets six times around.
    
        
    Post
    The body of a stitch: two legs that show as a V on the right side.
    
        
     Front loop
    The strand of the V lying on top of a stitch that is nearer to you.
    
        
     Back loop
    The far strand of that top V. A normal stitch goes under both loops.
    
        
    Working loop
    The one live loop on your hook. It never counts as a stitch.
    
        
    Marker
    The teal ring in the model sits in the first stitch of the round you are on. Move yours up every round.
